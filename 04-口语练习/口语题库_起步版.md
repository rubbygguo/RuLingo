# 口语题库：起步版

## 30-60 秒题目

1. How do you usually manage your study time as a postgraduate student?
2. Why can seminar participation be difficult for international students?
3. What is one academic skill you want to improve this month?
4. Do you prefer studying alone or with classmates? Why?
5. What makes a reading difficult for you?
6. How do you usually prepare for a presentation?
7. What is one challenge of studying in Hong Kong?
8. How do you deal with pressure before an exam?

## 2 分钟题目

1. Describe a course that has changed the way you think.
2. Talk about a time when you had to work in a group.
3. Explain one habit that helps you learn English.
4. Discuss whether postgraduate students should focus more on theory or practical skills.

## 通用结构

```text
My first point is that...
Another thing worth mentioning is...
For example...
That is why I think...
```
